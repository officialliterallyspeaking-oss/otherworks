HB.1349 — 2024 House text • BILL, not an Act
A Bill for an Act to alter the provisions of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 to provide for seat reservation for women in the National and State Houses of Assembly; and for related matters.
Sponsored by Rt. Hon. Benjamin Okezie Kalu

Working transcription of clauses 1–8 and the memorandum; not the later reported 157-seat version. Source: https://placng.org/i/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/HB.1349.pdf

ENACTED by the National Assembly of the Federal Republic of Nigeria as follows –

1. The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (in this Bill referred to as “the Principal Act”) is altered as set out in this Bill.

2. Section 48 of the Principal Act is altered by substituting for section 48, a new section “48” as follows –
“48. Composition of the Senate
(1) The Senate shall consist of –
(a) three Senators from each State and one from the Federal Capital Territory; and
(b) one additional Senator for each State and for the Federal Capital Territory, who shall be a woman.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1) of this section, nothing shall prevent a woman from contesting for any of the Senatorial seats under subsection (1)(a).
(3) The provisions of subsection (1)(b) shall commence after the life of the current National Assembly and shall be reviewed sixteen years after its commencement.”

3. Section 49 of the Principal Act is altered by substituting for section 48, a new section “49” as follows –
“49. Composition of the House of Representatives
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Constitution, the House of Representatives shall consist of –
(a) three hundred- and sixty-members representing constituencies of nearly equal population as far as possible, provided that no constituency shall fall within more than one State; and
(b) one additional member for each State of the Federation and the Federal Capital Territory, who shall be women.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1) of this section, nothing shall prevent a woman from contesting for any of the seats in the Federal constituencies referred to in subsection (1)(a).
(3) The provisions of subsection (1)(b) shall commence after the life of the current National Assembly and shall be reviewed sixteen years after its commencement.”

4. Section 71 of the Principal Act is altered by –
(a) inserting immediately after 71(a) and (b), a new subsection “(2)”–
“71. Senatorial districts and Federal constituencies
(2) For the purpose of –
(a) section 48 (1) (b) of this Constitution, there shall be established an additional Senatorial seat by the Independent National Electoral Commission to be occupied by a woman; an
(b) section 49 (1) (b) of this Constitution, the Independent National Electoral Commission shall divide each State of the Federation into two Federal Constituencies to be occupied by women.”; and
(b) renumbering the section appropriately.

5. Section 77 of the Principal Act is altered –
(a) in subsection (1) by inserting after the word, “constituency”, the words, “or special constituency”; and
(b) by inserting a subsection (3) as follows –
“(3) In this Part of this Chapter, “special constituency” means constituency specifically reserved from which only women shall be elected from in accordance with the provisions of Sections 48 and 49 of this Constitution.”

6. Section 91 of the Principal Act is altered by substituting the existing section 91 with a new section “91” as follows –
“91. Composition of the House of Assembly
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Constitution, a House of Assembly of a State shall consist of –
(a) three or four times the number of seats, which that State has in the House of Representatives divided in a way to reflect, as far as possible, nearly equal population; and
(b) one additional member from each of the three senatorial districts in the State under section 48(1)(a) of this Constitution, who shall be a woman:
Provided that a House of Assembly of a State shall consist of not less than twenty-four and not more than forty-three members;”
(2) Notwithstanding the provision of subsection (1) of this section, nothing shall prevent a woman from contesting for any of the seats in the Federal constituencies referred to in subsection (1)(a) of this section.
(3) The provisions of subsection (1)(b) shall commence after the life of the current State Houses of Assembly and be reviewed sixteen years after its commencement.”

7. Section 117 of the Principal Act is altered –
(a) in subsection (1) by inserting after the word, “constituency”, the words, “or special constituency”; and
(b) by inserting a subsection (3) as follows –
“(3) In this Part of this Chapter, “special constituency” means constituency specifically reserved from which only women shall be elected from in accordance with the provisions of Section91 of this Constitution.”

8. This Bill may be cited as the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (Alteration) Bill, 2024.

EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM
This Bill seeks to alter the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 to provide for seat reservation for women in the National Assembly and State Houses of Assembly. It seeks to remedy the low representation of women in Legislative Houses by providing for special constituencies to be contested and filled by women as a temporary measure to promote women’s political representation. The provision is subjected to a review after four general election cycles of sixteen years for the purpose of either retaining, increasing, or abolishing the temporary measure.
