Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (Fourth Alteration, No. 16) Act, 2017

1. The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (in this Act referred to as “the Principal Act”) is altered as set out in this Act.

2. Section 137 of the Principal Act is altered, by inserting, after subsection (2), a new subsection “(3)” -
“(3) A person who was sworn-in as President to complete the term for which another person was elected as President shall not be elected to such office for more than a single term”.

3. Section 182 of the Principal Act is altered, by inserting, after subsection (2), a new subsection “(3)” -
“(3) A person who was sworn-in as Governor to complete the term for which another person was elected as Governor shall not be elected to such office for more than a single term”.
