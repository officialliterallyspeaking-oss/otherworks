Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (Fourth Alteration, No. 27) Act, 2017

1. The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (in this Act referred to as “the Principal Act”) is altered as set out in this Act.

2. Section 65 (1) (b) of the Principal Act is altered by substituting for the word, “thirty”, in line 2, the word, “twenty-five”.

3. Section 106 (b) of the Principal Act is altered by substituting for the word, “Thirty”, the words, “Twenty-five”.

4. Section 131 (b) of the Principal Act is altered by substituting for the word, “forty”, the words, “thirty-five”.
