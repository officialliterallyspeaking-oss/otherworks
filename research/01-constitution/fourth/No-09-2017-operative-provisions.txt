Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (Fourth Alteration, No. 9) Act, 2017

1. The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (in this Act referred to as “the Principal Act”) is altered as set out in this Act.

2. Section 134 of the Principal Act is altered –
(a) in subsection (4), by substituting for the word, “seven”, in line 2, the words, “twenty-one”; and
(b) in subsection (5), by substituting the word, “seven”, in line 2, the words, “twenty-one”.

3. Section 179 of the Principal Act is altered-
(a) in subsection (4), by substituting for the word, ‘seven’ in line 2, the words, “twenty-one”; and
(b) in subsection (5), by substituting for the word, “seven” in line 2, the words, “twenty-one”.

4. Insert, after section 225, a new section “225A” -
“De-registration of political party
225A. The Independent National Electoral Commission shall have power to de-register a political party for -
(a) breach of any of the requirements for registration;
(b) failure to win at least twenty-five percent of votes cast in –
(i) one State of the Federation in a Presidential election; or
(ii) one Local Government of the State in a Governorship election.
(c) failure to win at least-
(i) one ward in the Chairmanship election;
(ii) one seat in the National or State House of Assembly election; or
(iii) one seat in the Councillorship election.”
